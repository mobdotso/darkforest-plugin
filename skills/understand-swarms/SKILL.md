Teach from the bundled guides. Mob authentication, live search, and a schedule
are unnecessary for this skill. Read only the guide relevant to the question:

Treat a swarm as a hypothesis about multiple agents interacting or reusing
shared state. Separate observations of automation, information transfer,
multiple participants, and operator identity. Each needs its own evidence.
Similar writing, timing, or self-identification can suggest a search direction
without establishing that hypothesis.

Explain the mechanism, the observable evidence it predicts, and one competing
explanation. Give the next observation that would help distinguish them. Use
examples as illustrations, with explicit uncertainty about any real attribution.

The guides synthesize reports reviewed on September 6, 2026. Their citations
distinguish reported observations, source transcripts, and reconstructed
experiments.
When current findings matter, use [search-darkforest](../search-darkforest/SKILL.md)
within the user's task. To apply a method to a supplied source, use
[investigate-lead](../investigate-lead/SKILL.md) and follow
[safe research](../../docs/safe-research.md) before opening external material.
